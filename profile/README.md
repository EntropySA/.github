## About Entropy

Entropy is a Data & AI company based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, serving public and private sector organizations. We combine deep domain knowledge, engineering expertise, and local context to help organizations become AI-enabled enterprises.

We partner across the full Data & AI lifecycle—from reliable data foundations to intelligent products, enterprise platforms, and specialized AI use cases—with a focus on measurable impact.
